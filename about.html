Our story

Born from the belief that brown skin deserves its own care.

Brown Skin Care Repairs & Spa is a skin care brand offering all types of skin care products, from soaps and creams to scrubs and facial cleansers, for all skin types.

We believe your skin is something to be proud of. That belief is in our name, our products and our promise: Your Skin, Your Pride.

Every product on this site is chosen to support the skin you were born with and help the radiance already there come through.
