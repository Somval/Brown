Protection

Why sun protection matters for every skin tone

2 min read

Melanin gives brown skin some natural protection from the sun, but not complete protection. Sun exposure can also darken existing dark marks and make skin tone look uneven.

Make it a daily habit

Apply sun protection to exposed skin every morning and reapply as directed on the product label. Choose a texture you enjoy, because the best sun protection is the one you will actually use.

Do not forget the extras

Hats, sunglasses and shade all help, especially in the middle of the day.
