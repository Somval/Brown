FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Add products to your bag, then check out. You can also order directly with us on WhatsApp.

How can I pay?

Checkout uses Paystack for secure online payment. If you prefer to order on WhatsApp, message us and we will guide you.

Do you deliver outside Lagos?

We deliver across Nigeria and worldwide. Delivery fees and times depend on your location, so message us before you order if you would like an estimate.

Which products are right for my skin type?

Our range is made for all skin types. Each product page shows the recommended skin type, and you can message us for help choosing.

Can you help me build a routine?

Yes. Follow the four steps of cleanse, treat, nourish and protect, or message us your skin concerns and we will suggest products.

How do I book a treatment?

Message us on WhatsApp or call 0808 503 0533 with the service you would like, and we will confirm availability with you.

A product I want is sold out.

Message us on WhatsApp and we will check availability for you.
